Game Rules – How to Play

  • Board: Numbers 1 to 100. Reach 100 to finish.
  • Wheel: Tap the coloured wheel to spin. The number under the top arrow is your result (1–6).
  • Start: You must spin 1 or 6 to enter the board. Spinning 1 moves you to square 1 (no extra turn). Spinning 6 moves you to 6 and gives an extra spin.
  • Turns: Players take turns spinning the wheel. Move forward by the number shown under the arrow.
  • Extra turn: Only a spin of 6 gives another spin.
  • Exact finish: You need an exact spin to land on 100.
  • Special squares (Green = up / Red = down):
    5 → 12 (Birthday), 13 → 7 (Study), 18 → 29 (Adult), 22 → 10 (Job), 30 → 54 (Marriage), 35 → 59 (Parents), 43 → 25 (Responsibility), 55 → 23 (Loan), 60 → 45 (Retirement), 65 → 80 (Grandparents), 71 → 84 (Grandkids), 75 → 52 (Alone), 89 → 23 (Health), 100 = Rest in Peace.
  • Tip: Tap any coloured (green/red) square on the board to read its instruction.
  • Modes: Multiplayer (2–4 players) or 1 Player vs Computer.
  • Menu: Restart, Pause, Game Rules, Close.
